DATE FIVE · THE HOTEL CHAPTER

A different place, the same feeling.

The fifth date changed the setting. Instead of our familiar Pizza Dot Com routine, we had the hotel, an evening outside, a walk and burgers waiting somewhere nearby.

the photos from this day

These are the little pieces of this date that I want to keep close.

What I remember

The evening felt softer with you in it.

There was something lovely about seeing you during the evening and walking beside you without having to rush through a plan. You looked beautiful, especially in that quieter atmosphere, and I loved having you next to me.

We didn't need an elaborate itinerary. We just walked, found somewhere to eat, talked and enjoyed being away from everything else for a while.

A detail I want to keep

Sometimes a change of scenery is enough to make an ordinary evening feel completely new.

The detail I want to keep is the evening walk after leaving the hotel. The change of scenery made everything feel slower, and I loved having you beside me without needing to rush anywhere.
