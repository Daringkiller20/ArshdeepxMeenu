Begin with us
01 · before everything

It started with the wrong person.

You messaged me first on Instagram after getting my ID through someone else. At the time, the account had also been used by my friend, so you thought you were messaging someone connected to that story.

You were looking for someone else. Somehow, you found me instead.

02 · the accident that mattered

Funny how life works.

The friends who unknowingly connected our paths aren't part of our lives anymore. But the connection they accidentally created stayed.

A wrong assumption became a conversation. The conversation became flirting. And the flirting became something neither of us expected.

03 · “we'll only be friends”

That promise lasted a few days.

In the beginning, you were very clear: we were only going to be friends.

Then came a few days of flirting.

And eventually, you asked me if I was actually serious about being in a relationship with you.

I was. And that was when we officially became us.

04 · the girl I got to watch grow

You became more you.

When I look at the early photos and compare them with the person I see now, I don't see someone who became a completely different person.

I see someone who slowly became more confident, more expressive, more comfortable, and more willing to let the world see who she really is.

And one of my favourite things about loving you is that I got to be there while that happened.

06 · the first four

Pizza, stolen hours & you.

On those first four dates, you somehow managed to make time for me around your clinical duty. You would come meet me, we'd spend our time together, and then you'd return to the hospital when it was time for the bus.

I don't remember those days because they were extravagant. I remember them because you were there.

07 · one tiny moment

And then you kissed me.

Our second date holds one of those memories that sounds small when you tell someone else about it, but never felt small to me.

The first time our lips met wasn't some dramatic movie scene. It was just a little peck from you.

Just a peck. Somehow, still one of the moments I'll remember for a very long time.

08 · then came the hotel chapter

Sometimes the place didn't matter.

For the later dates, we stayed at a hotel, went out for evening walks, found a nearby spot and ate burgers.

It wasn't about doing something extraordinary. It was about walking beside you, looking at you, talking to you, and getting to have those hours together.

09 · the little things

I know the small version of you too.

The girl who loves drawing, even though she hasn't drawn anything in a while. The girl who used to have endless things to say about her principal.

The girl who can be on her phone while we're video calling, answer me perfectly, and somehow make me wonder whether what I said went completely over her head.

Those little habits are part of the person I love too.

10 · the imperfect parts

We're not perfect.

Sometimes I get jealous. Sometimes I get overprotective. Sometimes I get irritated and say things more harshly than I should.

I know there are moments where I need to do better. Loving you doesn't mean I get to control you. It means I need to be honest about what I feel while still respecting your choices and trusting you.

I don't always get that right. But I want to keep learning.

11 · what you mean to me

You mean more to me than these photographs can show.

I love you for the big things, but also for the tiny things that probably wouldn't make sense to anyone else.

I love the way it feels when you're beside me. I love looking at you and knowing that somehow, out of all the people in the world, I get to call you mine.

I love the memories we've already made, and I love knowing there are so many ordinary days left for us to turn into memories too.

12 · to be continued

This isn't the end of our story.

These are only the memories we've collected so far.

And if all of this came from one accidental Instagram message, I'm pretty happy to see what comes next.

I love you, Meenu.
